What is happening?

Waiting for runtime measurements…

  1. Read one nuScenes sample and its six camera, LiDAR and radar records.
  2. Preserve sensor timestamps and transform point sensors into the sample’s reference ego frame.
  3. Convert camera BGR → RGB; resize to 448 × 256; convert to float [0,1]; normalize using ImageNet mean/std; arrange CHW.
  4. Batch six cameras through one shared pretrained ResNet-50 in eval + inference mode.
  5. Reduce 2048 backbone channels to 256 with a frozen averaging 1×1 projection. This projection is fixed, not trained.
  6. Cache the [1,6,256,8,14] tensor. Mean activation is mean(abs(feature)) across channels; Channel shows one of 256 activations.
  7. Normalize each displayed map to its own min/max. Colors do not identify objects or semantic classes. The 8 × 14 cells are shown without inventing detail.
  8. Re-render the chosen panel and stream it over WebRTC. Video refresh does not rerun inference.

dt is sensor capture-time offset relative to the selected nuScenes sample/reference time. Batching the images for neural inference does not remove this temporal skew.

LiDAR and radar remain raw/geometric. No learned BEV, depth, detection, or other Stage 4 processing exists.
